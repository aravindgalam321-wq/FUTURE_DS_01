# FUTURE_DS_01
This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship.
DATASET USED:
Restaurant / Coffee Shop Sales dataset from Kaggle
Tools Used:
Excel Web
Power BI
Project Tasks
Cleaned and organized sales data
Analyzed sales and revenue trends
Compared product performance across purchase types
Identified top-performing managers and cities
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard
Generated business recommendations based on sales insights
Key Insights
Burgers and Beverages generated high overall sales and order volume
In-store purchases contributed more orders compared to Drive-thru and Online modes
Credit Card payments were the most commonly used payment method
Cities like London and Lisbon recorded strong sales performance
Some managers consistently handled higher order volumes than others
Online purchase trends were lower for certain product categories, showing growth opportunities
Product demand varied significantly based on purchase type and payment method
Quantity sold and revenue were strongly connected across top-performing products
Business Recommendations
Increase focus on high-performing products like Burgers and Beverages
Improve online ordering strategies to boost digital sales performance
Offer targeted promotions for low-performing purchase channels
Strengthen customer engagement in top-performing cities to maximize repeat sales
Optimize staffing and inventory based on manager-wise and city-wise demand trends
Encourage digital payment offers to improve transaction efficiency
Use sales trend analysis to prepare inventory during peak demand periods
Introduce combo offers and seasonal campaigns to increase average order value
Dashboard Features
Product-wise sales analysis
Purchase type comparison
Payment method analysis
City-wise performance tracking
Manager-wise sales overview
Interactive visual reports and charts
